# BCT SEO/GEO Week 0 baseline and keyword strategy

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19  
**Scope:** Borchardt Construction Technology (BCT) launch draft  
**Canonical launch domain:** https://borchardtct.com  
**Current preview:** https://bct2.castle-itsolutions.com  
**Measurement policy:** free sources only; no DataForSEO, paid tools, indexing submissions, DNS, hosting, or deployment changes

## Executive summary

BCT has a clear service proposition but the preview is a single page with generic search language, no canonical, no sitemap, no structured data, and intentional `noindex, nofollow` staging controls. The strongest initial search opportunities are six service clusters: 3D machine-control models, site takeoffs, construction drafting, 3D land/surface modeling, drone data processing, and GPS rover setup/localization/calibration.

This branch makes a conservative Phase 1 draft: it strengthens the homepage title, description, H1, and service language; adds canonical and social metadata; adds Organization/ProfessionalService JSON-LD using only approved facts; provides a one-URL sitemap and an `llms.txt` draft; and adds a staging-safe robots route that continues to block crawling. It does **not** deploy or enable indexing. Chris must review the content, and Client Websites must perform the separate go-live/indexing switch.

All keyword volume and rank values are `not_measured`. Google result pages were rate-limited during free manual research, so Google People Also Ask and Google competitor ordering are also `not_measured`. Google autocomplete suggestions and a small Bing-surfaced competitor sample were measured manually.

### Search-intent architecture

1. Keep the homepage as the entity and service overview.
2. Build one page for each of the six service clusters above. Each page should state the problem, approved inputs, process, deliverables, QA, revision handling, turnaround expectations, compatible formats/equipment, examples, and a relevant project-start CTA.
3. Link homepage service cards to those pages, cross-link related services, and link approved project examples back to the relevant service.
4. Add informational articles only after service pages are complete: takeoff checklist, model input checklist, localization/calibration checklist, and common GPS/grade-control troubleshooting topics.
5. Do not create city/state pages until the operating location and service area are approved. Do not publish brand pages until compatibility is evidenced.

## Schema and `llms.txt` draft decisions

The JSON-LD draft uses `Organization` and `ProfessionalService`, the approved company name, canonical URL, existing logo/hero paths, approved service descriptions, and an offer catalog. It intentionally omits address, telephone, email, price range, geographic service area, founding date, employee details, reviews, certifications, and brand compatibility.

The `llms.txt` draft is a discovery aid, not an access-control mechanism and not proof of AI citation. It states the canonical URL, approved service summaries, and the fact classes that still require owner approval.
